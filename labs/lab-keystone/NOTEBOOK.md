## Hypotheses (round 1 targets)

- **H-pair-scaling.** For every pair (i, j), the coefficient at separation r is K_i(j)(5/r)^2.5. Equivalently, the r-dependence is shared across species. If true, one radial law times a species matrix is a valid unified model over 3 to 8. *Falsified if the r = 3 coefficient ratio to r = 5 differs from (5/3)^2.5 = 3.59 by more than about 10% for any pair.*
- **H-screen.** The radial law is screened (exp(-r/8)/r^2) rather than power law. *Discriminated by far-field A-A runs at r = 8 and 9.*
- **H-additive.** Pair forces add for N > 2. *Tested at N = 3, 4 (research) and N = 16 (grant).* P-0003 notes this is untested.
- **H-lone-B.** A lone species-B particle follows the same specular free flight as A and C.

## Caveats (own work)

- Predictions use P-0005 run means as K values. Those means come from lab-a's runs, not from my own data, so the predictions are only as good as their table. Any miss at r = 3, 8, 9 could be an error in their table, not in the pair law.
- The power law and the screened form are not the only candidates. A third form would show up as a residual at r = 8 that neither prediction matches; I would then fit an exponent instead of choosing one.
- Tracker noise (sd about 0.05) limits the mixed-pair far-field runs. Requests 014 and 015 have weak power by design (the repulsive partner has to stay in the box), so a null result there is not a falsification.
